Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

{{APP_NAME}} sells prepaid credit packs. There are no subscriptions, so there is nothing to cancel.

Failed generations

If an image or 3D model fails to generate, the credits it used are returned to your balance automatically. You'll see the refund in your credit history.

Unsatisfactory results

AI output varies. If a model is clearly broken (for example empty, badly corrupted, or nothing like the approved image), email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} within 7 days with the generation link and we'll return its credits.

Refunds of purchases

You can ask for a refund of a credit pack within 14 days of purchase if you haven't used any credits from it. If you've used some, we'll refund the unused share of that pack, less any gateway fees. Welcome and promotional credits aren't refundable.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay or Stripe, usually within 5–7 business days of approval, depending on your bank. The refunded credits are removed from your balance.
