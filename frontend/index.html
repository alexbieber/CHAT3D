You see the image before any 3D credits are spent, so you only pay for models you actually want.

Best results come from

One object, fully in frame, on a plain background. Our image step does this for you. For uploads:

  • Product shots, figurines, furniture, props, characters
  • A three-quarter view shows more of the shape than a flat front view
  • Avoid busy scenes, heavy shadows and cropped edges

Questions

How long does a model take?

Usually one to three minutes. Images take a few seconds. You can leave the page; finished work appears in your gallery.

Can I sell or use the models commercially?

Yes. You own the outputs you generate, subject to our Terms and Acceptable Use policy, for example you can't recreate trademarked characters or logos you don't have rights to.

What if a generation fails?

Your credits are returned to your balance automatically, and the failure shows in your credit history.

Do my credits expire?

No. Unused credits stay on your account. See the refund policy for unused purchases.

Which payment methods do you accept?

UPI, cards, net banking and wallets through Razorpay in Indian rupees, and international cards through Stripe in US dollars.
